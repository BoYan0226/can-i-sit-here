Can I Sit Here?

A staircase takes us from one place to another.

But sometimes, we stop halfway.

We sit beside a friend, set down a bag, and start talking.

The steps become a place to stay.

Nothing has been added. Nothing has been rebuilt.

A different use begins with the simple act of sitting down.

Across the city, people make these small adjustments.

A low wall becomes a seat. An edge becomes a meeting place.

What was already there begins to offer something more.

Can I Sit Here? begins with noticing these moments.

The city gives us spaces, and we give them uses.

Sometimes, a place changes when we decide to stay.
